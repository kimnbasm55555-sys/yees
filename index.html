Three days, three regions of the ear

Day one begins at the Eustachian tube and the middle ear: cholesteatoma, tympanoplasty, ossiculoplasty, stapes and inner ear surgery, all through the endoscope.

Day two follows the facial nerve from the geniculate ganglion to the stylomastoid foramen and opens the translabyrinthine route to the internal auditory canal.

Day three completes the infratemporal fossa approach: facial nerve rerouting, control of the internal carotid artery, exposure of the jugular foramen, the petrous apex and the clivus.
